Procedures Submitted to Medicare Part B by Advanced Practice Providers between 2010 and 2025

Calculate the number and proportion of procedures submitted to Medicare Part B by advanced practice providers (physician assistants, nurse practitioners, certified registered nurse anesthetists, anesthesiology assistants, certified clinical nurse specialists, and certified nurse midwives) between 2010 and 2025. The denominator includes claims submitted by advanced practice providers and physicians.

Enter up to twenty-five HCPCS procedure codes separated by semicolons.
